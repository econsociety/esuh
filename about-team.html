Isaac and Serena are economics students at the University of Houston, dedicated to conducting rigorous research across various areas of economics. Through their work with ESUH, they explore important economic questions and contribute to academic discourse.

Isaac

Isaac is an economics researcher with interests spanning labor economics, macroeconomic policy, and quantitative analysis. He brings a data-driven approach to understanding complex economic phenomena and enjoys working on both theoretical and empirical research projects.

Research Interests: Labor Economics, Macroeconomics, Econometrics

Serena

Serena is an economics researcher focused on development economics, international trade, and policy analysis. She is passionate about understanding how economic policies impact communities and exploring solutions to pressing economic challenges.

Research Interests: Development Economics, International Economics, Public Policy

Our Approach

Our research methodology combines theoretical frameworks with empirical analysis. We believe in:

  • Rigorous Analysis: Applying sound economic theory and statistical methods
  • Evidence-Based Conclusions: Drawing insights from data and empirical evidence
  • Clear Communication: Presenting research in an accessible, well-structured format
  • Continuous Learning: Engaging with current literature and refining our methods

Collaboration

We welcome opportunities to collaborate with fellow students, researchers, and faculty members. If you're interested in our work or would like to discuss potential collaboration, please feel free to reach out through ESUH.
