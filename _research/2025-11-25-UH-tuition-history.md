## Abstract

This working paper analyzes the University of Houston's resident tuition trends over time, examining how tuition costs have evolved in both nominal and real terms. The analysis provides insights into tuition affordability and helps contextualize current costs within historical patterns.

## Key Findings

This initial installment establishes the baseline data for the UH Inflation working paper series. Future installments will explore:
- Comparative analysis with peer institutions
- Relationship between tuition increases and educational outcomes
- Impact of state funding changes on tuition policy

## Methodology

The analysis uses publicly available data from the University of Houston's historical tuition records, adjusted for inflation using the Consumer Price Index (CPI).

## Conclusion

Understanding tuition trends is crucial for assessing higher education affordability and planning future policy interventions. This working paper series will continue to track and analyze these trends.
