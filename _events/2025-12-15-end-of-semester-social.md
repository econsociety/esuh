## End of Semester Celebration

Join us for a fun evening to celebrate the conclusion of Fall 2025 semester and recognize the achievements of our members!

### What to Expect

- **🍕 Food & Refreshments** - Pizza, snacks, and drinks provided
- **🎮 Games & Activities** - Board games, trivia, and economics-themed challenges
- **🏆 Awards Ceremony** - Recognition of outstanding members and contributors
- **📸 Photo Opportunities** - ESUH group photos and memory making

### Awards Categories

We'll be recognizing members in several categories:

- **Outstanding Member of the Year**
- **Best Research Contribution**
- **Most Active Participant**
- **Excellence in Leadership**
- **Rising Star Award** (for new members)

All ESUH members are eligible. Winners will be selected by the officer team based on participation, contributions, and engagement throughout the semester.

### Schedule

- **7:00 PM - 7:30 PM:** Arrival, food, and socializing
- **7:30 PM - 8:00 PM:** Games and activities
- **8:00 PM - 8:30 PM:** Awards ceremony
- **8:30 PM - 9:00 PM:** More games and networking

### Important Notes

- This event is for ESUH members only
- Please bring your student ID for entry
- If you have dietary restrictions, email us at esuh@uh.edu by December 10th

### RSVP

Please RSVP by December 12th so we can plan for food accordingly!

We look forward to celebrating with you!
