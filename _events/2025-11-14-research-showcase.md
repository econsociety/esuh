## Event Recap

Our fall research showcase featured five original research presentations by ESUH members. Topics ranged from housing affordability to the economics of student debt. Faculty from the UH Department of Economics provided feedback and moderated a lively discussion.

## Presentations

1. **The Economics of Finals Week** - Isaac Turner
2. **Housing Affordability in Houston Metro** - Sarah Chen
3. **Student Loan Debt and Early Career Decisions** - Marcus Williams
4. **Trade Policy Effects on Texas Agriculture** - Ana Rivera
5. **Gig Economy Labor Markets** - Jordan Patel

## Outcomes

Two of the presented papers were selected for further development as part of ESUH's working paper series.
