## Event Recap

We kicked off the fall 2025 semester with an exciting general meeting! Over 40 students came together to learn about ESUH's plans for the year, meet fellow economics enthusiasts, and hear about upcoming research and networking opportunities.

## Highlights

- Introduction of new executive board members
- Overview of fall semester events and research initiatives
- Open discussion on topics members want to explore
- Pizza and networking after the presentations

## What's Next

Stay tuned for our upcoming speaker series and research workshops throughout the semester.
