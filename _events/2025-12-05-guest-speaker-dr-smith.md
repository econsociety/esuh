## Guest Speaker Event

We are thrilled to announce that Dr. Sarah Smith, Associate Professor of Economics at Rice University, will be joining us for a special lecture on labor economics.

### About Dr. Smith

Dr. Sarah Smith is an accomplished economist specializing in labor markets, wage inequality, and workforce development. Her research has been published in top-tier journals including the *American Economic Review* and the *Quarterly Journal of Economics*. She has also advised several policy organizations on labor market reforms.

### Topic: The Future of Work

In this talk, Dr. Smith will explore:

- Recent trends in the labor market post-pandemic
- The impact of automation and AI on employment
- Wage inequality and policy responses
- Career paths for economics students interested in labor economics
