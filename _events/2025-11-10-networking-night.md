## Economics Career Networking Night

Our Fall 2025 Networking Night was a huge success! Over 50 students connected with economics professionals from various industries.

### Event Highlights

This year's networking event featured:

- **15+ Employers and Alumni Representatives**
  - Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas
  - JPMorgan Chase
  - Shell Oil Company
  - Deloitte Consulting
  - Houston Independent School District
  - Various UH Economics alumni working in diverse fields

- **Career Panels**
  - Financial Services Panel
  - Public Policy and Government Panel
  - Consulting and Analytics Panel

### Student Testimonials

> "I made valuable connections and learned about internship opportunities I didn't even know existed!" - Maria G., Economics Major

> "The alumni panel was incredibly insightful. It's great to see what UH economics graduates are doing in the real world." - James T., Mathematics & Economics

### What We Learned

Key takeaways from the event:

1. **Internship Experience is Crucial** - Nearly all employers emphasized the importance of internships
2. **Quantitative Skills Matter** - Strong data analysis and statistical skills are highly valued
3. **Network Early** - Building professional relationships during college pays dividends
4. **Economics Opens Many Doors** - Our alumni are working in diverse fields from finance to education to technology

### Thank You

A huge thank you to all the professionals who took time out of their busy schedules to meet with our students, and to everyone who attended!
