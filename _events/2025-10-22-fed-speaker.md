## Event Recap

ESUH hosted Dr. Maria Martinez, a Senior Economist at the Federal Reserve Bank of Dallas, for a talk on current monetary policy challenges and career opportunities in central banking.

## Key Takeaways

- The Federal Reserve's dual mandate of price stability and maximum employment shapes everyday policy decisions
- Data analysis and econometrics skills are highly valued in central banking roles
- Internship programs at regional Fed banks are a great entry point for economics students

## Q&A Highlights

Students asked about the impact of AI on economic forecasting, the Fed's approach to digital currencies, and practical advice for breaking into policy-oriented economics careers.
