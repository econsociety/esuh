## About This Event

Our Fall 2025 general meeting is an opportunity for all ESUH members to come together, network, and learn about what's ahead for our organization. Whether you're a new member or a returning officer, we'd love to see you there!

### Agenda

1. **Welcome and Introductions** (6:00 PM - 6:15 PM)
   - Meet our officers and new members

2. **Semester Recap** (6:15 PM - 6:30 PM)
   - Review of Fall 2025 activities and achievements

3. **Spring 2026 Planning** (6:30 PM - 7:00 PM)
   - Upcoming events and opportunities
   - Research initiatives
   - Guest speaker series

4. **Q&A and Networking** (7:00 PM - 7:30 PM)
   - Open discussion and networking time

### What to Bring

- Your student ID for building access
- Questions or ideas for ESUH activities
- Enthusiasm for economics!

### Refreshments

Light refreshments will be provided.

We look forward to seeing you there!
