Welcome

Welcome to the ESUH Economics Research portfolio, a collection of research papers and working papers by economics students Isaac Turner and Serena Emeonye at the University of Houston. This platform serves as a centralized archive for our research across various topics in economics.

Our research spans multiple areas of economics, including labor economics, macroeconomics, development economics, and more. Each paper reflects our commitment to rigorous analysis and evidence-based conclusions.
