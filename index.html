Welcome

Welcome to the Economics Research portfolio for the Economics Society at the University of Houston (ESUH), a collection of research papers and working papers by economics students Serena Emeonye and Isaac Turner at the University of Houston. This platform serves as a centralized archive for our research across various topics in economics.

About the Project

This portfolio represents the collaborative effort of ESUH (Economics Students at the University of Houston) to conduct meaningful research and contribute to the broader economics community. Learn more about our research team and the ESUH organization.
