ESUH (Economics Students at the University of Houston) is a student organization dedicated to fostering economic research, discussion, and academic excellence among undergraduate and graduate students at the University of Houston.

Our Mission

ESUH aims to:

  • Promote rigorous economic research among students
  • Provide a platform for students to present and discuss their research
  • Foster collaboration between students, faculty, and the broader economics community
  • Support students in developing research skills and professional competencies
  • Create opportunities for networking and academic growth

Research Portfolio

This website serves as a permanent archive and showcase for research conducted by ESUH members. By maintaining this portfolio, we aim to:

  • Document the research contributions of our members
  • Make research accessible to the broader academic community
  • Demonstrate the quality and breadth of student research at UH
  • Provide a resource for other students and researchers

Research Areas

ESUH members conduct research across a wide range of economic topics, including but not limited to:

  • Macroeconomics: Economic growth, business cycles, monetary policy
  • Microeconomics: Consumer behavior, firm theory, market structures
  • Labor Economics: Employment, wages, labor markets
  • Development Economics: Economic development, poverty, inequality
  • International Economics: Trade, finance, globalization
  • Public Economics: Taxation, public goods, government policy
  • Econometrics: Statistical methods, causal inference, data analysis

Get Involved

If you're a University of Houston student interested in economics research, we encourage you to get involved with ESUH. Whether you're just beginning your research journey or already have working papers in progress, there's a place for you in our community.

For more information about joining ESUH or contributing research to this portfolio, please contact the organization through the University of Houston's student organization directory.
