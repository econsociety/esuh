The Economics Society at the University of Houston (ESUH) is a student organization dedicated to fostering economic research, discussion, and academic excellence among students at the University of Houston.

Research Portfolio

This website serves as a permanent archive to showcase the research conducted and collected by ESUH Researchers. By maintaining this portfolio, we aim to:

  • Make research accessible to the broader academic community
  • Demonstrate the quality and breadth of student research at UH
  • Provide a resource for other students and researchers

Research Areas

ESUH members conduct research across a wide range of economic topics, including but not limited to:

  • Macroeconomics: Economic growth, business cycles, monetary policy
  • Microeconomics: Consumer behavior, firm theory, market structures
  • Labor Economics: Employment, wages, labor markets
  • Development Economics: Economic development, poverty, inequality
  • International Economics: Trade, finance, globalization
  • Public Economics: Taxation, public goods, government policy
  • Econometrics: Statistical methods, causal inference, data analysis

Get Involved

If you're a University of Houston student interested in economics, we encourage you to get involved with ESUH. Whether you're an econ major or not, there's a place for you in our community.

For more information about joining ESUH, please contact the organization through the University of Houston's student organization directory.
